OVERVIEW

What this service covers

A company list is more useful when it includes relevant roles. Decision-maker research focuses on job functions and professional information that match the client’s target audience.

Typical deliverables

  • Target company and website
  • Relevant role or job title
  • Public professional profile where available
  • Department or seniority, when clear from the source
  • Research notes for ambiguous matches

Who this service can help

B2B providers and teams targeting founders, owners, managers, department heads, or other defined professional roles.

What to include in your brief

  • Target companies or industries
  • Roles and seniority to prioritize
  • Locations
  • Fields that are essential versus optional
Important note

The right contact may not be publicly listed at every company. Matches are based on available information and agreed criteria.

How the process works

We first confirm the target criteria, required fields, expected format, and timeframe. Once the scope is clear, research and organization can begin, followed by your review of the agreed output.
