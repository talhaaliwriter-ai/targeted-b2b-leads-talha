Frequently asked questions

Need to clarify something before starting? Here are common questions about research scope and delivery.

What type of B2B leads can you research?

Research can focus on companies and relevant professional roles within an agreed industry, location, and set of criteria. Available fields vary by source and market.

Can I choose the countries, cities, or industries?

Yes. Provide the target country, region, city, industry, and any other requirements so the scope can be reviewed before work begins.

Can I request specific decision-maker job titles?

Yes. You can specify roles such as founders, owners, directors, sales leaders, operations managers, or other relevant positions.

Do you include email addresses and phone numbers?

These fields may be included when available from the researched sources and appropriate to the project. Coverage varies, so specific availability cannot be guaranteed for every record.

Can you clean or format a spreadsheet I already have?

Yes. Share a description of the file, the issues to address, and the format you need. The scope can include column formatting, data standardization, and duplicate review.

Which file formats can you deliver?

Common formats include Excel (.xlsx) and CSV. Other formats can be discussed depending on your workflow and project requirements.
