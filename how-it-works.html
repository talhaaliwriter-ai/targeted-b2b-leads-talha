HOW IT WORKS

A clear process from brief to delivery

Understand the steps, agree the scope, and know what to expect before research begins.

Home/How it works
01

STEP ONE

Share your target

Describe the businesses, audience, roles, or data you need. Mention the locations, industries, and use case so the research can be scoped appropriately.

  • Target industry and business type
  • Location or geographic coverage
  • Required columns and approximate volume
02

STEP TWO

Confirm the scope

Agree which fields are essential, which are optional, how the file should be formatted, and whether a small sample should be reviewed first.

  • Define inclusion and exclusion criteria
  • Agree file format and delivery expectations
  • Clarify what cannot be guaranteed
03

STEP THREE

Research and organize

Gather information from accessible sources within the agreed scope, keep the structure consistent, and flag uncertain or unavailable fields instead of inventing details.

  • Use consistent column names
  • Review duplicates as agreed
  • Keep source links or notes if requested
04

STEP FOUR

Review the files

Receive the agreed file type, review whether it matches the brief, and share any specific changes that are within the agreed scope.

  • Excel or CSV, as agreed
  • Clear notes about missing fields
  • Direct communication about adjustments

What to include in your first message

A brief description helps avoid unnecessary back-and-forth. You do not need a perfect document—answer what you already know.

  • Which service you need
  • Country, city, or target market
  • Industry, job titles, or business types
  • Required fields and approximate list size
  • Preferred delivery format and deadline
