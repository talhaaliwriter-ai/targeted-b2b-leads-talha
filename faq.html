Frequently asked questions

Practical answers about services, data availability, delivery, and getting started.

Home/FAQs
Which services are available?

Services include B2B lead generation, Google Maps business lead research, LinkedIn lead research, business data research, decision-maker research, Excel data cleaning, spreadsheet formatting, and custom lead lists.

Can I request specific columns?

Yes. Share required and optional fields in your brief. The scope can be confirmed before work begins.

Will every record have an email address and phone number?

No. Details depend on what businesses or professional profiles make publicly available. Missing details should be left blank or marked for review, not invented.

Are the sample leads on this website real?

No. The sample rows are illustrative examples used to demonstrate spreadsheet structure. They are not verified leads or claims about real businesses.

What file format can I receive?

Excel or CSV can be agreed based on how you want to review and use the research.

How do I request a quote?

Use the contact form to prepare an email or WhatsApp message. Include the service, target market, required fields, approximate volume, and deadline if known.

Does the website form store my details?

No. The static form opens your email application or WhatsApp with a prepared message. It does not send the form to a website server.

Can you guarantee replies or sales from a lead list?

No. Research can help organize a prospecting list, but replies, conversions, and sales outcomes depend on many factors outside the research itself.
